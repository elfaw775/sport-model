# Nested cross-validation on the archived 84 films

This analysis reuses all previously examined films. It produces internal out-of-fold estimates. The existing 59/13/12 experiment and its head remain separate. Patient identifiers and acquisition sites have not been independently verified. Folds are stratified by film label.

1. Generate five outer folds for each shuffled seed (42, 73, 101) in original manifest row order. Within each outer training subset generate four shuffled stratified folds using seed 1000 + outer seed + zero-based fold number. All methods receive identical partitions.
2. Compare seven frozen encoders fitted with identical image-level probes. Tie order is DINOv2 Small, RAD-DINO, ResNet18, bone-siglip, BiomedCLIP, DINOv2-base with registers, ConvNeXt V2 tiny. The official YOLO11s COCO feature extractor has a separate probe and cannot win the HF selection. A height-only control uses distance from median negative training height, fitted separately in every inner and outer training fold. The final baseline predicts every film positive. Fine-tuned detectors that have seen any held-out films are ineligible.
3. Fit row L2 normalization, training-only dimensional standardization and the same class-balanced logistic regression (L2, C=1) as the historical experiment. Feature extraction is frozen and operates independently per film. Choose among HF encoders using largest pooled inner out-of-fold ROC-AUC, then average precision, then the above tie order. Select thresholds independently per method using inner OOF scores only. For sorted distinct scores, use midpoints between adjacent levels, plus immediately outside the endpoints. Maximize balanced accuracy then sensitivity then specificity then threshold. Scores at or above the cutoff are positive. This explores a new midpoint rule relative to the old inclusive observed-score grid.
4. Refit each probe on the outer train films and score the outer holdout. The primary nested HF prediction is the preselected encoder and threshold. Quantify each prespecified fixed encoder, comparators, and the nested pipeline on the same outer case indices; do not rank on an outer fold then score that winner on the same fold.
5. Primary descriptive table: one prediction per film from seed 42, including the original 59-training-film subset separately. Report paired correct/incorrect discordances and two-sided exact McNemar results against YOLO features and height only. Report 95% Clopper–Pearson intervals as descriptive film-count intervals (dependent CV fits, no site sampling). Show each repeat separately, never count three repeated predictions for a film as three independent cases. Report per-film decisions across seeds and selected encoders/thresholds by fold. ROC-AUC is calculated on pooled OOF scores; variation in score scales across fold models can affect it.
6. If a refit head is produced for inference, select its encoder and threshold by a separate four-fold stratified pass over all 84 (seed 1042), then refit on all 84. Do not quote its fitted accuracy or claim an untouched test. No fresh hospital or external-cohort evidence is provided by this experiment.
